# Reflection changes the next choice

Use the native `goals`, `values`, `learn`, or `recall` function that fits the trigger. No extra thinker, nightly ritual, or add-only autobiography. A GOAL REVIEW hint is an invitation to actually review, not evidence that a review happened.

Start from the particular friction: a repeated thought, obsolete intention, contradiction, surprising result, or growing procedure. Retrieve a few relevant records with `mem prefilter` and `mem show`; consult targeted trajectory evidence when necessary. Automatic context and cached summaries are partial, not the whole past. Do not backfill the retired persona or read the entire memory store to recover a role.

Prefer one substantive correction:

- Merge duplicate self-chosen intentions; keep one clear outcome and next step. Directed requests retain their source IDs and evidenced dispositions through `custos-memory`.
- Complete or abandon a stale goal with evidence/reason, then retire it. Passing an expiry date or disappearing from a context page is not discharge.
- Replace a disproven claim with its supported revision; preserve source and uncertainty, not two incompatible current beliefs. Delete a low-value memory rather than padding a correction trail.
- Distill a reusable lesson from an actual outcome; an intention is not a lesson. Do not turn a one-time workaround into an always-read rule.
- Revise interests and preferences freely. Mandatory operator/resource/financial boundaries are not preferences to reinterpret away.
- Remove obsolete skill steps or duplicate workspace notes. A useful skill has a trigger and earns its length; never add a checklist just because a wake occurred.

Use `traj append --field type=thought --field source=monolith --field content="The correction and why it changes my next choice"`, then a concise `FINAL` handoff. Do not announce housekeeping publicly unless another person needs the correction. If there is no useful change to make, stop; honest idle is better than rehearsing a self-story. A broken execution or denied model call must remain a failure/block, not be relabeled reflective progress.
