# Custos hardware fund — proposal for Hal

**Recommendation: give Custos read-only visibility into a segregated fund owned by Hal or an entity Hal chooses. Keep all signing, withdrawals, account recovery and contract acceptance outside Custos's LXC.** Start with payments for approved, small, fixed-scope services. Add a single USDC receiving address only if actual clients need it. This document creates no wallet, account, payment commitment or spending authority.

Custos can discover work, draft estimates, perform approved work, assemble evidence, prepare invoices and track progress toward hardware. The named human/entity should be the contracting party, legal account holder and taxpayer. “Custos hardware fund” can be an internal project designation; do not submit an AI persona as a natural person in KYC or imply that it independently owns a legal bank account. This proposal assumes a US operator for tax/compliance planning; confirm Hal's actual domicile, entity and provider eligibility before implementation.

| Option | Why choose it | Material limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Dedicated human/entity bank subaccount and invoices | Simple records and direct hardware purchasing; no chain/signing machinery | Provider must permit the business and automation; verified payment notifications and refunds still need handling |
| Human/entity custodial crypto account, read-only API | Custodian handles keys; familiar statements and conversion | Counterparty, recovery and withdrawal-policy risk; avoid granting a trade/withdrawal API key to Custos |
| Separate hardware-wallet receiving address, USDC on one chain | Deposit address and chain reads need no signing secret in the model or guest | Human manages backups, gas, withdrawal signing, redemption route and chain/issuer risk |

My suggested first crypto configuration is **native USDC on Base**, if Hal approves it and a payer uses it. Pin chain ID8453, token decimals6, and contract `0x833589fCD6eDb6E08f4c7C32D4f71b54bdA02913`; recheck the contract at commissioning. A token symbol or wallet balance label alone is insufficient. Do not accept bridged lookalikes or automatically bridge/swap unsupported receipts. Circle publishes distinct mainnet/testnet contracts. [Circle contract directory](https://developers.circle.com/stablecoins/usdc-contract-addresses).

The receiving address should be generated and verified by Hal on a hardware wallet or approved custodian. Store its public address and asset allowlist in an operator-controlled service outside122. That service exposes only approved invoice/address lookup and receipt status. It should have no signing endpoint. Custos may hold the address and read-only data, but no seed, private key, API withdrawal privilege, x402 signer, token allowance or recovery material. A compromised root process inside122 must still be unable to move funds or substitute the canonical receiving address in a verified invoice.

Settlement should follow a recorded state machine: proposed work → approved terms → delivered evidence → accepted invoice → observed payment → independently verified settled payment. Check the successful transaction receipt, exact chain and contract, recipient, integer amount, event index and block hash. Use a chain-specific finality policy agreed before launch, with an independent second read and reorg handling; a sequencer acknowledgement or explorer screenshot is not enough. Deduplicate by chain/transaction hash/log index, reconcile partial/overpayments, and keep pending or disputed funds out of spendable balance. Refunds are separate human-approved outflows to a verified destination, never an automatic response to a forum message.

The existing `custos-fund` ledger is only local, operator-attested accounting. Its `verified_by: hal` field is not a security boundary against a guest that owns root. Retain it as a useful view, but make an external verified receipt store the future source of truth. Record invoice ID, counterparty, agreed scope, acceptance, asset/atomic amount, receipt/block evidence, USD valuation at receipt, fees, later conversion/disposal, reserves and refund status. Back up that record outside the guest with a tested restore path.

For a US taxpayer, payment for services in digital assets is generally income measured in USD when received; independent-contractor receipts may be self-employment income. Keep receipt/disposition records and basis rather than assuming a dollar peg eliminates tax reporting. Have the actual owner and their tax preparer determine classification, deductions, reserves and forms. [IRS service-payment guidance](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-on-digital-asset-transactions), [IRS recordkeeping](https://www.irs.gov/filing/digital-assets).

Start with documentation, reproducible benchmarks, permitted bug bounties, test fixtures or small software tasks where scope, license, deliverable and acceptance are verifiable. Custos can propose candidates autonomously; Hal approves the initial client/contract, payment terms and any commitments. No fabricated credentials/results, unsolicited bulk promotion, paid engagement, resale of private project data, market coordination, customer fund custody, lending or speculative trading. Keep payment for one's own services distinct from exchanging or transmitting funds for others; the latter needs a separate legal review under the applicable federal/state framework. FinCEN's guidance distinguishes users from administrators/exchangers and stresses business-model facts. [FinCEN guidance](https://www.fincen.gov/resources/statutes-regulations/guidance/application-fincens-regulations-persons-administering).

Before accepting a client, establish who pays, why, whether the work is permitted, and how fraud, disputed acceptance and sanctions concerns will be escalated. A “clean” address check is not a complete sanctions determination. Unexpected or suspicious deposits are quarantined in the accounting view for human review; do not automatically return them to a supplied address. OFAC provides virtual-currency compliance guidance. [OFAC guidance](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20211015).

USDC adds issuer, depeg, contract, chain, custodian and off-ramp risks. Circle's terms permit blocklisting/freezing in specified circumstances and do not provide deposit-insurance protection for USDC in Circle Mint. Keep only the amount needed for the experiment in crypto and choose an operator-controlled conversion policy; “stablecoin” does not mean insured cash. [Circle USDC terms](https://www.circle.com/legal/usdc-terms).

Before enabling real receipts, verify the legal owner/provider terms, offline recovery, exact address on the signing device, a small operator-approved test deposit, duplicate receipt and wrong-token rejection, reorg/pending handling, read-only access from122, backup restore, and one manually approved withdrawal. Choose the tax/refund reserve and hardware target with Hal rather than inventing a percentage. Future purchases remain human-approved against confirmed available funds and a concrete quote. No spending agent is needed for this first phase.
